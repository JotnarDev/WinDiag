What we heard

The brief

A hardware diagnostics tool for Windows, for technicians who refurbish laptops. It should feel precise, modern and technical, away from medical imagery and Windows blue.

technicalprecisemodernefficient

Success: readable at 16 px in the taskbar, works in one colour, no clichés (gears, magnifying glasses, stethoscopes, Windows logos).

Final design

Keycap W

A keycap with a W: the keyboard test that opens the website, turned into the mark.

  • Recognisable at 16 px on dark and light taskbars
  • Cyan on Carbon: clear of the category's blues, oranges and greens
  • A thicker bottom lip makes it a key, without drawing a keyboard
